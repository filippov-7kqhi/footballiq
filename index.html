About FootballIQ

Coaching the Mental Game, Not Just the Physical

Founded by former college quarterback Trevor Banks, FootballIQ was built on a simple idea: reps build muscle memory, but film study and decision-making build winners. Every program blends on-field mechanics with real classroom-style game study.

  • Position-specific coaching for QB, WR, DB, and LB
  • Weekly film breakdown sessions included in every plan
  • Programs for youth, high school and prep athletes
  • Individualized development tracking every season
